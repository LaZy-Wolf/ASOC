# Hardware Replacement

Covers replacing hardware already issued — broken, lost, stolen, or out of refresh cycle. Issuing a
machine to a new joiner is covered by the laptop provisioning policy.

## Refresh cycle

Laptops are refreshed every **three years** from the issue date. The refresh ticket is filed
automatically 60 days before the anniversary; you do not need to request it. Refresh is not
conditional on the machine having failed.

Monitors, keyboards, and docks are replaced on failure rather than on a cycle.

## Broken hardware

File a P3 ticket describing the fault. Two paths follow:

- **In warranty** — vendor depot repair, 5–8 business days. A loaner is issued for the duration.
- **Out of warranty** — assessed against the cost of replacement. If repair exceeds 50% of
  replacement cost, the machine is replaced rather than repaired.

If the machine is unusable and the employee is blocked, a loaner is issued **same day** in staffed
offices and next business day for remote employees. Say explicitly in the ticket that you are
blocked — the ticket is triaged as a P4 otherwise.

## Lost or stolen

File a **P2 ticket immediately**, before anything else. Severity is P2 because it is a potential
data exposure, not because of the hardware cost.

1. IT support triggers a remote wipe.
2. The security team reviews access logs for the period since last contact.
3. For theft, a police report reference is required for the insurance claim, but the wipe does not
   wait for it.

Replacement is issued once the wipe is confirmed, typically within two business days.

## Accessories and peripherals

Standard accessories — dock, external keyboard, mouse, one external monitor, headset — are
self-service from the hardware catalogue with no approval, up to a per-employee annual allowance.

A second external monitor, standing desk, or ergonomic chair requires manager approval. Equipment
recommended by an occupational health assessment is approved automatically and does not count
against the allowance.

## Home office equipment

Remote employees receive a one-time home office allowance at hire and an accessory refresh every
three years, aligned with the laptop refresh.

## Returns and disposal

Replaced hardware is returned within ten business days. Drives are wiped to the standard destruction
policy and the machine is either redeployed as a loaner or sent for certified recycling. Employees
may not keep, buy, or donate replaced corporate hardware.
